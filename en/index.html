About Nondadon

A shochu collection app from Kagoshima.

Just snap a photo of the shochu you're drinking, and it joins your own personal collection, along with the name of the brand and the distillery. Nondadon turns the shochu you meet, so often forgotten after the night is over, into memories you can keep.

About Us

We are a team of six graduate students from Kagoshima University taking part in ISHIN 2026. With the help of local distilleries and restaurants, we are building an app that uses IT to energize Kagoshima's shochu culture.

A great shochu last night—
forgotten its name today?

At izakaya (Japanese pubs), we often see customers who come for new or limited-edition shochu and take photos with the bottle. The desire to collect and remember shochu is already there. But in an ordinary photo album, those encounters tend to be forgotten as soon as the night ends.

You can't recall the brand
When the evening is all about conversation, it's hard to remember exactly which brand you drank.
Photos get buried
Your photos aren't linked to the brand or distillery name, so you can't find them later.
You don't know your own taste
There's no easy way to keep track of how many you've tried, how you drank them, or which ones you liked.
No way into the story behind it
There are few chances to discover what makes each brand and distillery special, from ingredients and production methods to the land itself.

What You Can Do with Nondadon

Drink, snap, save. Keep a record of every shochu you meet, without changing the way you usually drink.

Taste Map

Record your impression with a single tap on two axes: "Light / Rich" and "Fruity / Sweet Potato." We also tried a 5-point rating scale and an aroma chart, but chose this format because anyone can use it without hesitation. Where you place each shochu also helps you find others that suit your taste.

Recommendations for YouIn Development

We are developing a feature that suggests shochu close to your taste, based on your past records. Beyond how you drink it and how it tastes, we are designing how your preferences relate to ingredients, koji, and distillation methods, with input from distilleries and restaurants.

To Shochu Distilleries and Restaurants

Nondadon aims to broaden the experience of drinking shochu and to build new connections between the people who make it and the people who drink it. The app is still a prototype, and that is exactly why we want its design to reflect the insights of the people who know shochu best.

What We Aim For

  • Brand and distillery names stay with your customers. "That bottle I had at that place" becomes a name they can ask for next time.
  • Feedback on how people drink and what they taste comes together. We are exploring ways to deliver drinkers' voices (how they drank it and how they felt about it) to the people who make it.
  • A record of a drink at your restaurant brings customers back. When customers look back at their calendar, the glass they enjoyed at your place stays with them as a memory.

How You Can Help

Share Your Insights

When you recommend shochu to a customer, what guides your choice? Please tell us how you think about it in practice: customer preferences, how to mix it, how flavor relates to production methods, and more.

Provide Brand Information

To make sure the app shows accurate information, we would like to use details provided by distilleries, such as ingredients, koji, distillation methods, and recommended ways to drink.

Try Our Prototype*coming soon

Try recording drinks at your restaurant or with your own evening drink. It would help us greatly to hear about anything that's hard to use, and how your customers react.

Give Us Your Honest Feedback

No comment is too small. For example, "This feature would make it easier to recommend at our restaurant," or "This wording could be misleading."

We will work with you in a way that doesn't add to your workload. Please feel free to get in touch.

Our Project at ISHIN 2026

ISHIN 2026 is a program led by Japan's Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI) to discover and develop young IT talent and entrepreneurs in regional Japan (the Kagoshima edition of the AKATSUKI Project). It is not just a business contest: it aims to create initiatives that could only be born in that region, building on its local industries, culture, and geography, in partnership with local companies, governments, and research institutions.

Within the program, we have set our own challenges under the theme of making Kagoshima's shochu culture more enjoyable and energizing it through IT, and we are developing the app around that theme.
